EXIF tools on iPhone

Built for Photo Library workflows: view every tag, edit what you need, remove what you don’t — on-device.

Why a native app

Browser tools are great for a quick single file. iPhone albums, HEIC library items, batch selection, and history need an app that works with your Photos library directly. EXIF+ reads photos through PhotoKit and rewrites them with ImageIO, on the phone (privacy notes).

What EXIF+ shows on iPhone

Open a photo from your library and the metadata is split into eight groups: Date & Time, Location, Camera, Camera Settings, Author & Copyright, Description, Image Information and GPS Details. The Photos app shows only a few of these; EXIF+ lists the rest, including the exact coordinates a photo carries. Step by step: view EXIF on iPhone.

Remove location and metadata before sharing

Photos taken with Location Services on store the place and time in the file. To share a clean copy: pick the photo in EXIF+, check what it contains, and remove all metadata in one action. The cleaned photo is saved as a new item in your library, and the original stays untouched, so you choose which one to send. Removing metadata from a single photo works without Premium. Guide: remove location from photos on iPhone.

Edit instead of remove

Sometimes you need to fix a field, not wipe it. EXIF+ edits the date, GPS location, camera fields, author, copyright and description. Typical cases: a wrong date after a camera clock reset, a photo to be tagged with your copyright, or a location corrected on a map. As with cleaning, edited photos are saved as new library items.

Free and Premium

Viewing, editing and removing metadata on one photo is free in the current version. Premium adds multi-select batch remove and batch edit, and sharing a stripped temporary copy straight from the detail screen. History keeps a before/after comparison for up to 50 photos.

What EXIF+ does not do

It does not edit video metadata, does not treat XMP as a first-class format (it focuses on EXIF, IPTC and GPS), has no cloud sync or accounts, and does not overwrite the original library photo. JPEG and HEIC are rewritten with ImageIO compression, so a cleaned file is not guaranteed to be byte-for-byte lossless. HEIC/HEIF and PNG keep their format when saved from the library; other types may export as JPEG. Requires iOS 17.0 or later.

FAQ

What iOS version is required?

iOS 17.0 or later.

Does EXIF+ delete the original photo?

No. Cleaned and edited photos are saved as new items; the original stays unless you delete it.

Is removing metadata on iPhone free?

For a single photo, yes. Batch remove and strip-on-share need Premium.

Does it work with HEIC photos?

Yes. HEIC/HEIF is preserved when saving from the library.

Can it remove metadata from videos?

No. Only photo metadata is supported.

Need this on iPhone?

EXIF+ views, edits, and removes EXIF/IPTC metadata — including GPS — on-device. Single-photo clean and edit are available to start; Premium unlocks multi-select batch tools and share-without-metadata. Cleaned photos are saved as new Photo Library items (originals stay).
