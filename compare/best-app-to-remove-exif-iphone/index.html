Best app to remove EXIF data on iPhone

The best app is the one that cleans the photo on your device, keeps the original, and does the job in few taps. Here are the criteria, the options, and where EXIF+ fits.

Updated 2026-11-06

Direct answer: Pick an iPhone app that removes EXIF and GPS on the device (no upload), saves the result as a new photo instead of overwriting the original, supports HEIC, and lets you clean several photos at once. EXIF+ does these things; the iOS share sheet can drop location only, and a browser tool is fine for a single JPEG, PNG or WebP.

Five criteria that matter

  1. Processing stays on the device. A photo you want to make private should not be uploaded to a server to be cleaned.
  2. Original is kept. The tool should save a new copy so a mistake cannot destroy your only photo.
  3. HEIC works. iPhone photos are usually HEIC; many browser tools cannot open them.
  4. Removes more than location. Camera model, software and date also describe you.
  5. Batch. Cleaning one photo at a time stops being practical after a few.

When EXIF+ fits, and when it does not

It fits if you have an iPhone or iPad (iOS 17+) and want to see, edit or remove EXIF, IPTC and GPS on photos in your Photo Library. Single-photo view, edit and remove are free; multi-select batch and strip-on-share need Premium. Cleaned photos are saved as new Library items and the original is kept.

It does not fit if you need to edit video metadata, read or write XMP, or work on a computer. For one JPEG, PNG or WebP on a computer, the browser EXIF remover is enough.

FAQ

What is the best app to remove EXIF data on iPhone?

Look for on-device processing, HEIC support, a saved copy instead of overwriting, and batch cleaning. EXIF+ is built around those.

Can I remove EXIF on iPhone without an app?

Partly. The share sheet's Options lets you turn off location for that send, but it does not remove camera and date tags from a saved file.

Is it safe to use an online EXIF remover?

Only if it processes the file locally. Check the tool's privacy statement; the browser tools on this site read files in your tab.

Does removing EXIF lower photo quality?

Stripping tags does not change the pixels, but an app that re-saves a JPEG or HEIC may recompress it. See the page on quality.

Need this on iPhone?
