Best EXIF viewer for iPhone

An iPhone EXIF viewer should open library photos directly, understand HEIC, show location clearly, and let you remove what it finds.

Updated 2026-11-06

Direct answer: The Photos app shows only a few details (date, camera, size, sometimes a map). A dedicated EXIF viewer such as EXIF+ lists the full EXIF, IPTC and GPS tags of a photo in your library, including HEIC, and lets you edit or remove them from the same screen.

What the built-in Photos app shows

Swipe up on a photo or tap the info button to see date, camera, lens and file size, and a location map if the photo has GPS. It does not list every tag and it cannot remove most of them.

What to look for in a viewer

  • Direct access to the Photo Library so you do not have to export first.
  • HEIC support, since most iPhone photos are HEIC.
  • GPS shown plainly, as coordinates or on a map.
  • An action next to the data: edit a wrong date, or remove location before sharing.

Computer or browser instead

For a JPEG, PNG or WebP on a computer use the browser EXIF viewer; it reads the file in your tab. For other options see EXIF viewer alternatives.

When EXIF+ fits, and when it does not

It fits if you have an iPhone or iPad (iOS 17+) and want to see, edit or remove EXIF, IPTC and GPS on photos in your Photo Library. Single-photo view, edit and remove are free; multi-select batch and strip-on-share need Premium. Cleaned photos are saved as new Library items and the original is kept.

It does not fit if you need to edit video metadata, read or write XMP, or work on a computer. For one JPEG, PNG or WebP on a computer, the browser EXIF remover is enough.

FAQ

Does iPhone have a built-in EXIF viewer?

The Photos info panel shows basic details such as date, camera and location, but not the full tag list.

How do I see all EXIF data on iPhone?

Use an EXIF viewer app. EXIF+ reads EXIF, IPTC and GPS from photos in your library.

Can an EXIF viewer read HEIC?

Many desktop and browser tools cannot. EXIF+ reads HEIC from the Photo Library.

Is there a free EXIF viewer?

Yes. The browser viewer here is free, and single-photo viewing in the app does not need Premium.

Need this on iPhone?
