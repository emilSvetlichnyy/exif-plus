How to preview Content Credentials before exporting in Photoshop

Photoshop shows a preview of what will be attached when you export. Here is where to look.

Updated 2026-11-19

Direct answer: In Photoshop, open File → Export As and review the Content Credentials options in the lower-right of the dialog. The Preview tab of the Content Credentials panel lists what would be attached, such as identity details, connected social accounts, edit history and ingredients. Choose None to attach no Content Credentials. Menu names can differ by version, as Adobe still labels the feature Beta in places.

FAQ

Where do I preview Content Credentials in Photoshop?

In Export As, check the Content Credentials options at the lower right; the panel's Preview tab shows what will be attached.

How do I export without Content Credentials?

Choose None in the Content Credentials option.

What can Content Credentials include?

Identity details, connected social accounts, edit history and image ingredients, depending on your settings.

Are Content Credentials the same as EXIF?

No. They are a separate signed record (C2PA) from the EXIF tags in the file.
