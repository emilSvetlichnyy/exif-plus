Does AirDrop send photo location?

By default, assume yes. AirDrop sends the photo itself, and the share sheet's Options decide what travels with it.

Updated 2026-11-19

Direct answer: AirDrop transfers the photo file, so location can go with it. On recent iOS versions the share sheet has an Options button at the top where you can turn Location off for that send. Whether you rely on the default or the switch, the safest option is to send a cleaned copy.

Before you AirDrop

  1. Select the photo, tap Share.
  2. Tap Options at the top and switch Location off.
  3. Choose the AirDrop recipient.

FAQ

Does AirDrop send GPS location?

It can. AirDrop sends the file; use the share sheet's Options to turn Location off, or send a cleaned copy.

Where is the Location switch?

Tap Share on a photo, then Options at the top of the share sheet.

Does the switch remove camera and date tags?

Do not assume so. It targets location; use a metadata remover for the rest.

Does the switch change my original photo?

No. It only affects what is sent.
