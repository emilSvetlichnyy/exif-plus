What is IPTC data?

IPTC is the descriptive layer of photo metadata: who made it, what it shows, and who owns it.

Updated 2026-11-19

Direct answer: IPTC data is photo metadata defined by the International Press Telecommunications Council. It holds text a person writes about an image: caption, headline, keywords, creator, credit and copyright notice. EXIF, by contrast, is mostly technical data written by the camera.

IPTC fieldExample
HeadlineHarbour at sunrise
Description / CaptionFishing boats leaving the harbour at 6 a.m.
Keywordsharbour, boats, sunrise
CreatorPhotographer's name
Copyright Notice© 2026 Name
Date Created, City, Country2026-11-02, Lisbon, Portugal

Why it matters

IPTC can contain your name and location in words, not only in coordinates, so a GPS-only cleanup does not remove it. EXIF+ reads EXIF, IPTC and GPS and can edit or remove them. See EXIF vs IPTC, batch edit copyright and the glossary.

Sources: IPTC Photo Metadata Standard. Checked 19 Nov 2026.

FAQ

What does IPTC stand for?

International Press Telecommunications Council.

What is IPTC metadata used for?

Captions, keywords, creator, credit and copyright text, mainly for publishing and archiving.

Is IPTC the same as EXIF?

No. EXIF is mostly camera-written technical data; IPTC is descriptive text added by people or software.

Can IPTC reveal my name?

Yes, if a Creator or Copyright field is filled in.
