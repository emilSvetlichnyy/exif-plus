Do screenshots have metadata?

A screenshot usually carries much less than a camera photo — no lens or GPS from a camera — but it is not guaranteed to be empty.

Updated 2026-10-12

Direct answer: Screenshots typically do not include the GPS coordinates or camera settings a photo has, because nothing was captured by a camera. They can still include a creation date, the device or software name, and dimensions. The pixels themselves can also reveal more than any tag.

What a screenshot may contain

  • Date and time created, and image dimensions
  • Device or software information, depending on the system
  • Text visible on screen — names, messages, addresses — which is the bigger privacy risk

How to check

Drop the file into the image metadata viewer. If any field is sensitive, strip EXIF online. For an iPhone screenshot in your library, use view EXIF on iPhone.

Before you share one

Crop out notification banners, account names and addresses first. Removing metadata does not hide what is visible in the image.

FAQ

Do screenshots have EXIF data?

Often a little, such as date and device software, but usually no camera settings and no GPS.

Can a screenshot show my location?

Not through GPS tags in most cases, but visible content on screen can.

How do I check screenshot metadata?

Open the file in a metadata viewer and read the listed fields.

Should I remove metadata from screenshots?

It is a cheap extra step, but cropping private details matters more.
