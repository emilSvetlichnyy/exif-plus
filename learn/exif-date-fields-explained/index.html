EXIF date fields explained

DateTimeOriginal, CreateDate and ModifyDate are three different clocks. Here is which one answers which question.

Updated 2026-11-19

Direct answer: DateTimeOriginal is when the picture was taken. CreateDate (also called DateTimeDigitized) is when it was stored as a digital file, usually the same moment on a phone. ModifyDate is when the file was last changed by software. The date in your file manager is a separate file-system date, not EXIF.

Why photos sort in the wrong order

Library apps usually sort by DateTimeOriginal. If it is missing, they fall back to another date, and photos from messengers or downloads can jump to the day you saved them. Fixing the shot time puts them back in sequence.

Privacy angle

Shot time plus GPS shows when you were at a place. If you do not want that, remove the dates along with location. To correct a wrong date in the app, see change photo date on iPhone; EXIF+ edits the date fields and saves the result as a new library item, keeping the original.

FAQ

What is the difference between DateTimeOriginal and CreateDate?

DateTimeOriginal is when the photo was taken; CreateDate (DateTimeDigitized) is when it was stored digitally. On phones they are normally the same.

Does editing a photo change its EXIF date?

Editing software usually updates ModifyDate and may leave DateTimeOriginal alone.

Why is my photo dated today?

The file-system date changes when you save or download a copy. The shot time is in EXIF, if it is still there.

Can I change the date a photo was taken?

Yes, by editing DateTimeOriginal. EXIF+ lets you do this on iPhone.
